              How to play
              1. One player is secretly the Fake — they get only the category, not the word.
              2. Players take turns adding one line each to the shared drawing.
              3. Everyone votes for who they think is faking it.
              4. If the Fake is caught, they get one guess at the word.
              Rounds

              Play as many rounds as you like (set in room settings). Each round brings a new word, a new category and — by default — a new Fake, so nobody can coast on one lucky role.

              Scoring
              • Fake caught + wrong guess → every artist +1
              • Fake caught + right guess → Fake +2
              • Fake escapes → each Fake +2

              Tap an emoji at the bottom any time to react. Works best with 3–10 players on their own phones.
